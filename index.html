Bio

Learning by mapping.

Photo neededYou, in the field

I'm an Agricultural Business Management student at NC State, working at the intersection of GIS, agriculture, and civic technology.

I've published StoryMaps in marine science, urban history, political simulation, and food systems — some in front of educators at national conferences, others in a middle-school classroom in Wake County.

Outside the classroom I led the National 4-H Geospatial Leadership Team and run a small GIS consulting practice focused on civic and agricultural clients.

Field Notes

Photos and sketches from those memo books are pinned to the map here, tied to wherever they were actually written.
